# Founding Partner Pilot: agreement summary

*A plain-language summary of what we both agree to. Not legal advice; have it reviewed before relying on it.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Service** | Estimate Recovery: follow-up on stale, unsold quotes, done for you |
| **Length** | 14 days from the day I receive the quote list |
| **Scope** | 25 quotes, unsold, 14–60 days old, $8,000+ |
| **Price** | Either **$300 flat** (invoiced at kickoff), **or $0 upfront + $500 per closed job** (see below). Chosen before kickoff. |

## How it works

1. **Contractor provides** the 25 quotes (estimate number, first name, scope, amount, date, notes) or restricted access to the CRM or estimate inbox to read them.
2. **Contractor confirms** that each homeowner requested an estimate and may be followed up by the agreed channels (email and/or text), and lists anyone to skip.
3. **Operator drafts** follow-ups and sends them to the contractor as one numbered list.
4. **Contractor approves** each batch by reply. **Nothing is sent without approval.**
5. **Messages are sent from the contractor's own email or business number**, so homeowners reply to the contractor.
6. **Warm leads:** when a homeowner wants to talk, the operator texts the contractor a handoff (quote number, first name, what they said, suggested next step). The contractor calls back, ideally within one business day.
7. **At the end:** a one-page report of what was sent, replies, handoffs and outcomes.

## Ground rules

- At most three short follow-ups per homeowner over the 14 days (for example day 1 email, day 4 text, day 9 email). Only channels the contractor approved.
- Any opt-out or "please stop" ends contact with that homeowner immediately and permanently, and the contractor is told.
- No pressure tactics, no discounts or promises the contractor hasn't approved, no statements about what insurance will cover.

## Data and privacy

- By default the operator keeps **only** estimate number, first name, scope, amount, age and notes. Phone numbers, emails and addresses stay in the contractor's system.
- The quote list is used only for this pilot and isn't shared with anyone else.
- When the pilot ends, the operator deletes the pilot records on request, keeping only the list of opted-out estimate numbers so those homeowners are never contacted again.

## Pay-on-results terms (if chosen)

- **Closed job:** a homeowner from the 25-quote batch whom the operator handed over as a warm lead during the pilot, who signs a contract with the contractor within **60 days of that handoff**.
- The contractor tells the operator about each closed job. The operator shares the handoff list so both sides can check it.
- **$500 per closed job**, invoiced when the contract is signed, due within 14 days.

## Either side

Either side can stop the pilot at any time by message. If the flat fee was paid and the pilot is stopped by the operator before any messages are sent, it is refunded.

**Agreed by:** contractor (name, company, date) and operator (name, date). Agreement by reply email is fine.
