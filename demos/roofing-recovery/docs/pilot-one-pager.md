# Unsold-estimate follow-up pilot

*For independent roofing contractors. A draft to adapt before sending; the items in [brackets] are yours to fill in.*

## The problem

Most roofing businesses send more estimates than anyone has time to follow up. After the first call or two, open estimates quietly go cold. Some of those homeowners still need the work and would answer a polite check-in.

## What we do

We work through your unsold estimates and follow up for you, **using your name and only with your approval**:

1. **You send us an export** of open estimates (a spreadsheet is fine; we provide a template).
2. **We clean it up.** We remove duplicates, sold or lost jobs, anyone who asked not to be contacted, and anyone you want left alone.
3. **We check permission.** We only contact homeowners where you can show they agreed to be contacted on that channel (for example, the consent box on your estimate form).
4. **You approve every message** before it goes out. Nothing is sent without your OK.
5. **We follow up** with a short, personal sequence: an email, a phone call where permitted, and a final polite note.
6. **We hand you warm leads.** When a homeowner says the project is still open and asks for a callback, inspection or revised estimate, we pass them to you with notes. You take it from there.
7. **You get a report** showing what was reviewed, what was sent, who replied, and what happened.

## What we never do

- Contact anyone without your approval of the message.
- Contact anyone who has opted out. Opt-outs are recorded and respected.
- Pretend a reply is a sale. We report replies, appointments and won jobs separately, and won jobs only when you confirm them.
- Use your customer list for anything else, or share it.

## What we need from you

- An export of open estimates from the last [60–90] days, with contact details and any consent information you have.
- The name you want messages to come from, and a short note on your services and current availability.
- Someone who can approve messages within [1 business day].
- Prompt pickup of warm handoffs, ideally a call back within [1 business day].
