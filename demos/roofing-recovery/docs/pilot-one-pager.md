# 14-Day Founding Partner Pilot

**Estimate Recovery for residential roofers: turn stale quotes back into conversations.**

## The problem

Every roofing company has a pile of estimates that went quiet: waiting on the adjuster, getting a second bid, "talking it over". Nobody has time to chase them properly, so good jobs drift to whoever follows up next.

## The offer

For 14 days I personally work **25 of your stale quotes**: unsold, 14–60 days old, $8,000 and up. I work them **under your company name, with your approval on every message before it goes out.**

1. **You send me the 25 quotes.** An export, a photo of the list, or restricted access to your CRM or estimate inbox, whichever is easiest.
2. **I write the follow-ups.** Short, personal, no pressure, based on where each homeowner left off (insurance, second bid, budget, timing).
3. **You approve them by text.** I send one numbered list; you reply "Approved" (or "Approved except 3"). About 10 seconds.
4. **They go out from your email or business number**, so replies come straight to you.
5. **I hand you warm leads instantly.** When a homeowner wants to talk, you get a text: who, which quote, what they said, and what to do next.
6. **You get a one-page report** at the end: what was sent, who replied, and what came of it.

## Pricing: pick one

| Option | Cost | Best if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Flat pilot fee** | **$300**, covers setup and 2 weeks of dedicated outreach | You want a fixed, known cost |
| **Pay on results** | **$0 upfront + $500 per closed job** from the 25-quote batch | You only want to pay when it works |

A "closed job" is one of the 25 homeowners I handed to you as a warm lead who signs a contract with you within 60 days of that handoff.

## The zero-friction promise

- **No new software to learn.** You never log into anything. You approve by text and get warm leads by text.
- **Your customer data stays in your system.** With restricted inbox or CRM access, I don't export phone numbers, emails or addresses anywhere. I track each quote by its estimate number and first name only.
- **Nothing goes out without your OK.** Every message, every time.
- **Opt-outs are respected immediately.** Anyone who says stop is never contacted again, and anyone you want left alone (neighbours, disputes, friends) is skipped.
- **Instant SMS handoffs** when a homeowner wants to talk.

## What I need from you

- The 25 quotes: estimate number, first name, scope, amount, date sent, and any notes ("waiting on adjuster").
- Your confirmation that these homeowners asked you for an estimate and that following up with them is OK, plus anyone to skip.
- Approval replies within one business day, and a call-back to warm leads within one business day.
